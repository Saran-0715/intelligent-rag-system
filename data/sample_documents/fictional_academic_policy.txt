GREENWOOD UNIVERSITY — STUDENT ACADEMIC POLICY HANDBOOK (FICTIONAL)
Version 2024-01

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY

1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in all courses.
Attendance below 75% will result in the student being debarred from sitting
for the final examination in that course.

1.2 Medical Leave
Medical leave requires a medical certificate from a registered practitioner
submitted within 3 working days of return. Medically approved absences up to
10% of total classes may be granted on a case-by-case basis.

1.3 Attendance Recording
Attendance is recorded by faculty at the beginning of each class session.
Students arriving more than 15 minutes late will be marked absent.

SECTION 2: EXAMINATION RULES

2.1 Eligibility
To be eligible for final examinations, a student must:
(a) Maintain 75% or above attendance
(b) Have no outstanding library dues
(c) Have submitted all required assignments

2.2 Examination Conduct
Mobile phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside examination halls.
Possession of unauthorized materials during examinations is treated as malpractice.

2.3 Malpractice Consequences
Students found committing malpractice during examinations will:
(a) Receive a zero in that paper
(b) Be subject to a disciplinary committee hearing
(c) Potentially face suspension for one semester on first offense
(d) Potentially face expulsion on repeat offense

SECTION 3: GRADING SYSTEM

3.1 Grading Scale
S Grade: 90-100 marks (Outstanding)
A Grade: 80-89 marks (Excellent)
B Grade: 70-79 marks (Very Good)
C Grade: 60-69 marks (Good)
D Grade: 50-59 marks (Average — Minimum passing grade)
F Grade: Below 50 marks (Fail — Must re-appear)

3.2 Grade Point Calculation
GPA is calculated on a 10-point scale.
S grade = 10 points, A = 9, B = 8, C = 7, D = 6, F = 0.

3.3 Re-examination
Students who fail (F grade) may re-appear in the supplementary examination
held within 30 days of the original result declaration.

SECTION 4: DISCIPLINARY POLICY

4.1 Academic Misconduct
Academic misconduct includes plagiarism, fabrication of data, unauthorized
collaboration, and misrepresentation of credentials.

4.2 Plagiarism
Plagiarism is defined as the use of another person's work, ideas, or words
without proper attribution. Assignments flagged for >30% similarity using
plagiarism detection tools will be investigated.

4.3 Consequences
First offense: Written warning and zero marks for the assignment.
Second offense: Suspension for one month.
Third offense: Expulsion from the institution.

SECTION 5: FEE POLICY

5.1 Fee Payment Deadlines
All semester fees must be paid before the last date specified in the academic calendar.
Late payments attract a fine of Rs. 50 per day for the first 15 days.

5.2 Fee Defaulters
Students with fee arrears exceeding one semester will not be permitted to
attend classes or access university facilities until dues are cleared.
